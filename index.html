Project management evidence

Agile portfolio · Nexus Setups

This section summarises the five artefacts used to reflect on the Nexus Setups website project. The project was completed on 1 October 2026; the six-month timeline is presented as a retrospective summary rather than a claim that every original sprint date was recorded exactly.

6 monthsApproximate project duration
1 Oct 2026Completion date
5Agile artefacts
01 · Team charter

Shared goal and teamwork

The charter set a shared goal for delivering the website and highlighted the need for planning, communication, consistent design, and review. The main lesson was that teamwork matters because changes in one area can affect the whole website.

02 · User stories

Focus on visitors

User stories connected website tasks to visitor needs, including understanding the business, finding information, navigating sections, and seeing a consistent presentation. Clear acceptance criteria helped define what a useful result should look like.

03 · Product backlog

Prioritising the work

The backlog grouped the work around the website's purpose, branding, page structure, content, and usability review. In future projects, I would keep priorities updated and attach screenshots or links as evidence.

04 · Sprint backlog

Breaking work into phases

The retrospective timeline followed planning, design, development, refinement, and final review. Recording sprint goals, tasks, blockers, and review decisions as they happen would make future timelines more accurate.

05 · Agile logbook

Recording progress and learning

The logbook summarised the project's progression through the six-month period. The project reinforced that a website should be reviewed as one complete experience, with consistent notes and evidence captured throughout delivery.

Timeline note: The phases above are a retrospective summary based on the overall project period and completion date. Exact sprint dates should be aligned with original project records if they are available.
